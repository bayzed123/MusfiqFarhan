About Me

My Journey

Since 2018, I have been on an incredible journey in the entertainment industry. Starting with my television debut in "O Jeno Amar Hoy," I have successfully built a diverse portfolio across multiple mediums including television dramas, radio hosting, and digital content creation.

As an RJ, Actor, and Content Creator, I have worked with leading production houses and media platforms, bringing stories to life that resonate with audiences across Bangladesh. My work spans from intense dramatic performances to engaging radio content and viral digital media.

With over 7 million followers across social platforms, I am committed to creating meaningful content that entertains, inspires, and connects with people from all walks of life.

My Work

🎬 Television

Featured in acclaimed dramas including "Tipu Sultan," "Sheshta Tumi," and "Niyamat." Known for versatile roles ranging from intense villains to romantic leads.

🎙️ Radio Hosting

Engaging RJ with years of experience hosting popular radio shows. Known for connecting with listeners through compelling storytelling and entertaining segments.

📱 Digital Content

Active content creator with millions of followers. Produces engaging videos, behind-the-scenes content, and interactive digital media across multiple platforms.
