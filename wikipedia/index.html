Musfiq R. Farhan, also known to audiences as RJ Farhan, is a Bangladeshi actor, radio jockey and content creator. His work moves between intimate character stories and the energy of contemporary entertainment, and his natok and telefilm reach viewers across Bangladesh and the wider Bengali-speaking world.

Alongside screen acting he works in radio broadcasting, music videos and digital storytelling. This page collects what the official site publishes about his career in one reference view; the list of works below is drawn directly from the site's own published archive and updates as new titles are released.

Career

Radio

Farhan is widely known as a radio jockey, and the name RJ Farhan remains the one many listeners recognise first. Radio shaped the register he is known for on screen: a conversational delivery and a close, direct relationship with the audience.

Screen work

His screen work centres on Bangla natok and telefilm — the single-episode and short-serial drama form that anchors Bangladeshi television and, increasingly, YouTube-first release. Eid programming is a fixture of that calendar, and Eid specials make up a recurring part of his release schedule.

Digital and music video

Farhan also appears in music videos and publishes through digital platforms, where releases reach audiences directly rather than through a broadcast slot. The official site's archive covers teasers, poster releases, behind-the-scenes notes and full releases alongside the finished work.

Work and reception

The through-line across radio, natok and digital work is character and rhythm — stories built on how people actually speak to one another, and a delivery shaped by years of live broadcast. Audience response is recorded on the official site directly: every published page carries a rating and a note field, and the fan wall collects messages written to him.
