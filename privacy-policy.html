Privacy
policy.

This policy explains what information the official Musfiq R. Farhan website receives when you browse, submit a love note, contact the studio or use the publishing system.

Information you submit

If you submit an audience note, the site receives the name, rating and note you choose to provide. Notes are stored in the site’s dedicated Cloudflare database and remain unpublished until moderated. The site does not ask for a password, phone number or private social account.

Contact

If you email the studio, your message and email address are handled by the email service you use and by the studio for responding to your request. Do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the enquiry.

Technical data

The hosting, security and delivery providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser, device, request time and requested URL to deliver pages, protect the service and diagnose errors. The site may later use privacy-respecting analytics or advertising services; any such service will be subject to its own policy and applicable consent requirements.

Cookies and advertising

The public site does not require an account. If advertising, analytics, embedded video or consent-management tools are added, they may place cookies or similar technologies according to their settings. Users should review the relevant provider notices and consent controls when displayed.

Reviews and moderation

Audience notes may be rejected or removed when they contain abuse, impersonation, private information, spam, copyright violations or material unrelated to the site. Approved notes may be shown on the homepage Fan Love Notes marquee and review section.

Retention and requests

We retain submitted notes and contact messages only as long as reasonably needed for moderation, publication, support, legal compliance or site operations. To request correction or removal of a submitted note, contact farhanvengers.contact@gmail.com with the relevant name and page context.

Changes

This policy may be updated when site features, analytics, advertising or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
