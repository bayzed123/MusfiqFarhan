## Project Synopsis

**Doob** is a serene and captivating love story set against the beautiful backdrop of blue waters. The film explores the tender chemistry between two souls as they navigate the complexities of love and connection.

The story unfolds with poetic cinematography, capturing intimate moments and emotional depth. With its stunning visual narrative and powerful performances, **Doob** promises to be a memorable addition to Bangladeshi cinema.
