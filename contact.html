For work, press & collaborations

Reach the official studio directly for professional enquiries and corrections.

What to include

Please include your name, organisation or publication, project details, timeline, and budget or deliverables where relevant, along with the best way to reply. Press requests should include the intended publication and the deadline.

Corrections

If you notice an incorrect title, date, credit, link or image on this site, send the page URL and the proposed correction. The studio can update published metadata and content through the private publishing dashboard, and the modified date on the page will reflect the change.
